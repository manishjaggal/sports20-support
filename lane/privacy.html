Lane · Privacy

Your records stay with you.

Lane stores your app records on your device. It does not require an account or integrate advertising, analytics or tracking.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Scope and contact

This policy describes Lane and these support pages. For privacy questions or requests, contact powerpassive46@gmail.com.

Information stored in the app

Workout names, pool configuration, swim sets, timer state, notes and saved workout summaries. Personal notebook and optional backup files.

These records, including any personal notebook entries, are stored as JSON files in the app’s local Documents folder. They are not automatically sent to the developer. The app does not integrate a cloud-sync service, advertising SDK or analytics SDK, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Backups, sharing and deletion

You can export a full backup or share supported records using the native system share sheet. You choose the destination. Restore uses the system file picker and replaces the current records only after confirmation. Any service or person you share with handles that copy under its own practices.

Device backups may include app files. Use About & data to erase the active app records. Exported files, recovery copies and device backups may remain outside those active records; remove those separately through Files or your backup provider if you want to delete them. The developer cannot access or recover your local records remotely.

If you email support

When you email support, your email provider sends your address, message and any attachments to the support mailbox. That information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email is handled through the relevant email providers, including Gmail for this support address. Do not send information you do not want included in the conversation.

You may contact the same address to request removal of support correspondence. This request concerns the support mailbox; it does not remotely delete app data, copies in your own mailbox or provider-managed backups.

External services and links

The app can open your email app and system sharing or file interfaces when you choose those actions. These providers have their own privacy practices.

These support pages

These pages contain no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising, forms or site-set cookies. Fonts and app icons are served with the pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. That hosting activity is separate from the app’s local records.

Policy updates

If app data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date. Please contact support if you need clarification about the version you use.
