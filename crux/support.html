Climbing · Help & support

A little help with Crux.

Crux keeps your bouldering projects and the work behind them together. Add a problem, record each attempt and keep the beta you want to remember for your next session.

Email support

Getting started

Boulderers keeping a personal project journal. Projects > Add a boulder enters a route name, location and V grade. Open a route to log an attempt/send, edit details and add beta. Progress derives totals, hardest send, first-attempt sends and grade/style summaries from those entries. Library deletes routes with undo. Location is manually entered text, not device location data.

You can use the app without creating an account.

Keep a copy of your records

  1. Open About & data in the app. It is usually under More or a settings gear.
  2. Select Prepare full backup, then save or share the JSON backup using the system sheet.
  3. To import a saved backup, select Restore backup and choose the file. Confirm only when you want to replace the current app data.

Store a backup somewhere you can access before deleting the app or replacing your device. A backup contains your app records and notes, so share it only with a destination you choose.

Troubleshooting

If an entry looks wrong, use the relevant edit or undo control where available. If you see a storage warning, keep the original files and any recovery copies before reinstalling or erasing data.

For help, email the app name, what you were doing, what happened, and your iOS version. You may include a screenshot after removing details you do not want to share. Please do not send a full backup unless it is needed and you are comfortable sharing its contents.

What this app covers

Personal bouldering journal using V grades, without outdoor route maps or automatic movement analysis.

Your data, your choice

App records stay in local device storage. About & data includes an erase option. Copies you export or keep in device backups are managed separately.
